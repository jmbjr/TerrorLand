# TerrorLand Player Guide

> Working-title guide for the first playable prototype. Names and balance may change.

## What Is TerrorLand?

TerrorLand is a watchable action-RPG adventure. Your hero handles movement and combat, while you make the decisions that shape survival: which equipment to wear, how cautiously to use potions, when to risk another encounter, and when to spend a portal token to retreat.

You can actively manage every new item or let the expedition run while you watch your Vanguard carve through the Silt Tombs.

## Your First Character: The Vanguard

The Vanguard is a durable close-range fighter. They automatically choose a target, move into range, and swing their equipped weapon. Their performance depends heavily on equipment.

The Vanguard uses five core attributes:

- **Vitality** increases health and recovery.
- **Might** improves physical damage.
- **Guard** reduces damage and improves defensive equipment.
- **Tempo** helps the Vanguard attack and recover faster.
- **Fortune** improves the chance of finding unusual gear.

You do not need to understand every number before starting. Early on, the item comparison panel highlights the likely effect of an equipment change.

## Starting an Expedition

From Emberrest:

1. Review your equipment and backpack space.
2. Choose a potion policy and auto-equip policy.
3. Select **Begin Expedition**.
4. Watch the Vanguard enter the first floor of the Silt Tombs.

After you unlock deeper floors, you may resume from the deepest unlocked floor or begin again from floor one to gather more gear.

## Watching Combat

The Vanguard fights automatically. The expedition display shows:

- Your health and active effects.
- Current enemies and their health.
- Floor and encounter progress.
- Recent attacks and important combat events.
- Newly dropped loot.
- Your available portal tokens.

You may pause whenever you want to inspect equipment or read the full combat log.

## Influencing the Autoplayer

Your influence is deliberately limited but important.

### Potion Policy

- **Conservative:** saves potions until health is dangerously low.
- **Balanced:** uses potions at a moderate health threshold.
- **Desperate:** uses healing readily to reduce immediate risk.

The exact thresholds may change as the prototype is balanced.

### Auto-Equip Policy

- **Off:** every equipment decision is yours.
- **Higher Score:** equips unlocked items when their general score is higher.
- **Build Preference:** favors the attributes you select, even when another item has a slightly higher general score.

Lock a favorite item to prevent automation from replacing or discarding it.

### Retreating

If you possess a portal token, press **Retreat to Emberrest**. The Vanguard must complete a short channel before escaping. Taking a dangerous hit may interrupt the retreat, so do not wait too long.

Retreating preserves your acquired gear under the default rules. The token is consumed when the portal successfully opens.

## Understanding Loot

Defeated enemies and containers can produce weapons, armor, trinkets, potions, currency, and portal tokens.

Equipment has four initial rarity tiers:

- **Common:** dependable equipment with few or no special properties.
- **Tempered:** improved gear with additional modifiers.
- **Exalted:** rare gear with several strong modifiers.
- **Mythic:** exceptionally uncommon gear capable of shaping a build.

Rarity color is reinforced with labels and visual patterns. A rarer item is not automatically better for your current build; check its effect on damage, defense, and attack tempo.

## Managing Equipment

Open the equipment panel while the game is paused to:

- Compare an item with what is currently equipped.
- Equip or unequip it.
- Lock it against automatic replacement or disposal.
- Discard unwanted gear.
- Review how it changes key combat estimates.

Equipment slots include main hand, off hand, head, body, hands, feet, and trinket.

If the backpack fills up, the game follows your selected inventory policy. For the safest early experience, choose **Pause When Full**.

## Floors and Progress

The Silt Tombs contain five ordinary floors followed by the Mummy Queen’s chamber. Each floor contains a series of encounters. Clearing the required encounters unlocks the next floor.

Enemy groups become larger and more dangerous as you descend. Champion enemies may have unusual properties but offer better rewards.

If the Vanguard falls, the expedition ends and the character returns to Emberrest. The default prototype penalty removes progress on the current floor but does not permanently delete the character or their established equipment.

## The Mummy Queen

The sixth floor contains the prototype’s final boss. The Mummy Queen changes tactics as her health falls, calls reinforcements, and introduces a dangerous curse or arena hazard in her second phase.

Before entering her chamber:

- Make room in your backpack.
- Review your highest-impact equipment choices.
- Carry healing supplies.
- Keep a portal token if you want an escape option.

The first victory guarantees a boss-quality reward and displays a run summary.

## Winning the Prototype

Defeat the Mummy Queen to complete the current vertical slice. The victory summary records:

- Total expedition time.
- Enemies defeated.
- Deaths and retreats.
- Deepest floor reached.
- Notable items found.

Future versions may continue beyond the Silt Tombs, but the first prototype ends here.

## Quick Tips

- Pause after a colorful drop; it may change your whole build.
- General item score is guidance, not a command.
- A defensive upgrade can be more valuable than raw damage near a boss.
- Lock anything you do not want auto-equip or inventory cleanup to touch.
- Retreat before the Vanguard is one unlucky hit from defeat.
- Record a run’s seed when something surprising happens; it can help reproduce the same scenario during testing.
